# Evidence-gated recursive improvement

Canonical English. [한국어](evolution.ko.md). Apply with [common rules](../AGENTS.md) and [verification](verification.md). Public seed cases: [evaluation corpus](../evals/cases.md).

**EVO-001** Recursive improvement repeats a bounded propose/challenge/repair/recheck cycle over instructions and, only when separately in scope, consuming software. This package implements the procedure as Markdown, not a self-running agent or evaluator. It grants no extra authority to modify code, merge, deploy, or change goals. Proposals are review data; document inspection is not effect execution. Function recursion, fixed-point semantics, and iterative review are distinct; Haskell papers prove no convergence or agent-performance gain for this procedure.

**EVO-002** Freeze the parent revision, accepted requirements, rule IDs, review criteria, relevant cases/sources, and authorized scope before each candidate. For actual software also pin the language/toolchain and executable oracle. State one falsifiable hypothesis and a finite budget (default: three substantive repair rounds, unless the user sets another bounded budget). Record baseline outcomes and uncertainty. A budget limit never converts unresolved findings into success; documentation review does not need an invented build environment.

**EVO-003** Express module changes against small stable contracts and a pure reference relation. Change one cause at a time; compare public values, error/state/effect traces, and resource obligations. Compatible implementations may evolve recursively behind the contract. A contract change is a versioned migration with its own approval and downstream compatibility review, not a hidden refactor.

**EVO-004** Separate attacker and defender passes. For Markdown, identify exact rule wording, a concrete scenario, the incorrect decision it permits, and a rule/source-based rebuttal or repair. Trace the scenario before and after the repair; do not call that an executed test. For actual software, reproduce using its applicable gates. Recheck neighboring rules, loading paths, and translations after each repair. State whether review was one assistant's sequential passes, separate sessions, independent agents, or human review; do not fabricate independence.

**EVO-005** Freeze acceptance criteria and existing counterexample intent while judging a candidate. Do not delete inconvenient cases, lower obligations, change exclusions, or relabel missing evidence to obtain a pass. A user's explicit scope change may retire an out-of-scope delivery mechanism, such as a bundled checker: record that authorization, the enforcement lost, the retained obligations, and the new review method. It does not turn manual inspection into equivalent runtime evidence or weaken consuming-software gates. Correct a faulty oracle only with a separately recorded rationale/authorization; review both baseline and candidate under the same corrected criterion.

**EVO-006** Claims of improved coding-agent effectiveness require separately authorized matched evaluations, development cases and independent held-out tasks, repeated runs, and preserved results. Match model/settings/tools/repository/budgets; measure correctness, purity and authority violations, evidence honesty, unnecessary complexity, and cost. Compare costs only after required quality gates pass. Prose cases and document review are not executed LLM evaluations. Documentation correctness fixes can complete under CHK-014 with agent impact explicitly unmeasured. Do not generate a benchmark runner here or purchase evaluation access merely to maintain these instructions.

**EVO-007** A candidate can complete only within its authorization and with no unresolved findings or known required-contract regressions in the stated scope. Preserve counterexamples, inspected text/source evidence, outcomes, and candidate identity; include commands/results only when actually executed. Stop after a clean full scoped recheck, not after an arbitrary number of reassuring passes. Repeated candidates, no progress, or budget exhaustion with findings require a blocker report. Completing guidance review is neither permission to merge nor approval of any consuming implementation.

**EVO-008** Retain the parent revision, rollback plan, and Markdown record of hypothesis, evidence, decision, and limits. New evidence starts a new bounded cycle; append counterexamples and revisit affected callers, profiles, translations, source mappings, and acceptance criteria. Preserve prior findings in a dated or commit-linked history rather than presenting them as current certification. Synchronize bilingual rule IDs and meaning; IDs support traceability, not equivalence proof.

**EVO-009** For profile/common-policy review, reason through the applicable Markdown cases: C aliases/output writes, Rust interior mutability/implicit effects, eager actions, overflow, termination, unknown outcomes, evaluator gaming, and missing instructions. Include valid immutable closures and values to detect over-restriction. Derive each outcome from normative rules and primary sources; challenge the case's expected answer too. This review never admits a particular mutating kernel: FP-007 requires its own project-specific evidence and authorization.

**EVO-010** The maintenance ledger must distinguish facts from proposals: what ran, what was only inspected, what is out of scope, and what remains unverified. A clean scoped review is not a guarantee of all future code, a formal proof, or demonstrated coding-agent improvement. Do not publish benchmark wins without matched executions and preserved results.
