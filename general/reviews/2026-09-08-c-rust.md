OpenAI's guidance supports explicit scope, relevant context, and evidence-based performance claims, not mandatory bundled automation for prose maintenance.

## Final scope and limits

Two substantive repair passes were followed by a full-scope reread of the common rules, both language profiles, verification/evolution contracts, paired translations, cases, links, and evidence wording. The 30 listed scenarios produced no further unresolved document finding in that scope. This is a scoped textual-review result, not a numeric model benchmark or an absence-of-all-bugs proof.

No C/Rust program, sanitizer, formal prover, or model-comparison benchmark was executed in this cycle. Document existence, identifiers, links, and final diff were inspected; such structural checks do not prove translation semantics or enforce downstream purity. Actual client loading and actual consuming implementations remain unverified. A policy accepted here still does not approve an individual mutating kernel or authorize merging.
